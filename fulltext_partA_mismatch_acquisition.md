## Overall verdict (Part A): PARTIALLY TAKEN (adjacent), direct claim still open. Confidence: moderate, provisional given the partial arXiv-API coverage.
No paper found sweeps structural-mismatch strength ε and measures where residual- or uncertainty-guided acquisition for NN surrogates falls behind Sobol/LHS. The pieces exist separately:
- residual acquisition: Polanska;
- AL vs LHS/Sobol benchmarks: AL4PDE, OGAS;
- sequential design vs maximin under a missing-cubic Duffing: Zhu & Hainy (GP/KOH);
- BED degraded by discrepancy: Yang/Wu.

## Design recommendations that fall out of the reading
1. **Critical: the ε-blindness confound.** Suppose training labels come from the nominal simulator, and the acquisition scores (ensemble variance, nominal-PDE residual) depend only on the surrogate and the nominal simulator. Then the acquired design and the trained surrogate are *identical for every ε*. ε enters only at test time: err_ε = E‖f̂−f₀‖² − 2ε E⟨f̂−f₀, g⟩ + ε² E‖g‖², where f_ε ≈ f₀ + εg. The A-vs-B gap is linear in ε, so ε* = ΔMSE₀ / (2 Δ E⟨f̂−f₀, g⟩) can be computed analytically from one training run. Reviewers will see this. The gap also shrinks relative to the ε² floor, which is dilution rather than a "loss". Make ε affect acquisition:
   - (a) labels from the *true* system, with the residual computed against the *nominal* PDE. Residual acquisition then gets an irreducible ε·g floor and is pulled toward high-discrepancy regions, while ensemble variance is not. This is the non-trivial version.
   - (b) a multi-fidelity setting: cheap nominal-simulator calls plus a few true-system calls, with acquisition choosing the true-system queries.

   Either way, report the analytic decomposition as a diagnostic.
2. In setting (a) or (b), include a GP/KOH reference arm (GP emulator plus discrepancy GP with IMSPE or maximin sequential design, as in Zhu & Hainy) on S1/S2. Without it, a stat.CO reviewer will say this has been solved for GPs.
3. Baselines to copy from AL4PDE/OGAS: random, LHS, Sobol/maximin, SBAL (stochastic, not greedy Top-K only), LCMD; residual with Polanska-style nearest-neighbour normalisation. Note that under (a) this normalisation uses true-system trajectories and inherits the mismatch, which deserves an ablation.
4. Metrics: mean plus tail (p95/p99/max) error, as OGAS stresses. Mismatch effects are likely to appear in the tails first.
5. Cite 2608.13633 (misspecification review, no acquisition coverage) and 2610.27130 (residual acquisition backfiring) in the motivation.
