## 2. Clarifications (implementation of frozen definitions, not changes)

1. **Budget-AUC** (prereg-v1 section 7: "trapezoid on log error") is implemented as exp of the trapezoid average of log median error over n = 8, 16, ..., 56, so the analysis ratio in A1/A2, log(AUC_arm / AUC_Sobol), equals the difference in trapezoid-averaged log median error.
2. **Correction model for all RQ2 arms** is the small MLP discrepancy ensemble (3 hidden layers of width 32 as in the surrogate code, M = 5, 300 epochs, no held-out split because n starts at 8). Arm 6's GP is used only to score candidates, not to correct.
3. **Arm 9** (exploratory) scores its first 8 points against the uncorrected nominal surrogate.
4. **Fit seeds** of the correction ensemble are the same for every arm, so arms are compared on identical fits. Arm-specific randomness is limited to batch sampling and score models.
5. **RQ1 conventions not fixed by prereg-v1.** Gaussian intervals use 1.645 times the relative norm of sigma against the relative total error. Conformal intervals are normalised by ensemble sigma (score = relative error divided by relative sigma). Calibration points are the first 8, 16, 32 and 64 of one scrambled Sobol stream, observed with the frozen 1% noise; the nominal calibration uses 200 simulator points. MC dropout: dropout 0.1, 50 passes, one network.
6. **Event for AUROC**: relative total error above its 80th percentile within each cell (frozen).
7. **Semigroup signal** (v1.3, exploratory): not built; will be reported as "not run" unless built before the pilot.
8. **Conformal guarantee at 8 calibration points.** With the finite-sample rule the quantile index ceil((n + 1) x 0.9) is 9 for n = 8, which exceeds n, so the quantile is the sample maximum and the guarantee is 8/9 = 0.889, not 0.90. This is reported next to every n = 8 conformal result.
9. **Model seeds.** Network and fit seeds are derived from (seed, purpose, index) with `stream_seed`, so that no two networks share a seed across replications or roles; in the development scripts the nominal ensemble used `base_seed = seed` and the correction ensembles `seed x 10000 + n`, which collide across seeds (seed 8's nominal members equal seed 0's correction members at n = 8).

## 3. Seed streams

Every random stream is `stream_seed(seed, purpose, index)`, a hash of the replication seed, a purpose label (train, test, design, pool, noise, calibration) and an index. Pilot seed 0 and throwaway seeds 101 and 102 and main seeds 1-10 keep their frozen roles; only the derivation of data from them is specified. A runner refuses these seeds unless explicitly told otherwise.

## 4. RQ2: Family A as frozen, plus Family F

**Family A** (A1-A3 and the C1 gate of v1.1) runs unchanged: output-space correction, full 10 s horizon, arms 1-8 (arm 9 exploratory).

**Family F (new, 4 tests, its own Holm correction at alpha = 0.05).** Acquisition for a dynamics-space correction.

- *Correction model.* A small network h(phi, omega) added to the nominal acceleration, fitted to the observed noisy trajectories by gradient matching followed by 20 L-BFGS iterations of trajectory matching (code: `mismatch/dynamics.py`). Refitted after every round. Fit budget fixed in section 4a; other hyperparameters fixed in the pilot.
- *Arms.* 1 random; 2 Sobol; 3 nominal ensemble sigma; F5 disagreement among K = 5 learned terms (different seeds, same data), averaged over the candidate's trajectory; F6 state-coverage: the score of a candidate is the mean, over 40 equally spaced sample times, of the distance in the (phi, omega / 3) plane from the candidate's nominal-ODE state to the nearest state sampled from the observed trajectories so far (a space-filling rule in state space instead of parameter space). Only phi is observed, so omega for the observed trajectories comes from a smoothing spline (smoothing chosen by generalised cross-validation); the nominal-ODE states of candidates come from the RK4 solver. F5 uses the same fitted term as the correction plus K - 1 = 4 further fits with different seeds on the same data; the score is the mean over time of the across-term standard deviation of the rollouts. All arms use the same correction-fit seed in a given round (paired fits). Same initial 8 points, 6 rounds of 8, pools of 4,096 and batch rule as prereg-v1 section 7.
- *Fit budget (4a, author's decision, 2026-11-15).* Each correction is one fit with 20 L-BFGS iterations after 500 gradient-matching steps, with no restarts. The author chose this for cost, against the recommendation of 60 to 150 iterations with best-of-three restarts, after the dev-seed diagnostics in section 1 showed the 20-iteration fit is under-converged and that fits of identical data differ by up to 2 x. Consequences stated now: (i) fit noise is of the same order as the arm differences seen on the development seed, so F1-F4 may be unable to detect real design effects; (ii) a rough power guess, labelled as a guess: if the per-seed SD of G = log(AUC_arm / AUC_Sobol) is about 0.18 (fit noise only; design variance adds to it), 10 paired seeds and a Holm-corrected alpha of about 0.0125 give a minimum detectable effect of about 0.17 to 0.19 in log units, or 17-21%; (iii) the pilot measures, and does not tune, the fit-to-fit coefficient of variation (CV) of the learned-ODE error at n = 8, 24, 56 on both systems with three fit seeds each, and reports it with the results.
- *Scored quantity.* Median relative L2 error of the learned-ODE rollout against the noise-free true system on the test set, full 10 s; budget-AUC as in clarification 1. The corrected surrogate (nominal surrogate plus the learned ODE minus the nominal ODE) is reported alongside; on the development seed it sits at the surrogate's approximation floor, so arms cannot separate on it.
- *Cells.* S1 and S2 at 4 x eps_ref, n_nom = 512, seeds 1-10.
- *Tests.* F1-F2: arm F5 (term disagreement) vs Sobol on S1 and on S2. F3-F4: arm F6 (state coverage) vs Sobol on S1 and on S2. Paired t-test across seeds on G = log(budget-AUC_arm / budget-AUC_Sobol); supported only if Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 and the 95% BCa CI excludes 0 (frozen decision rule).
- *Stated stop rule.* If no arm beats Sobol in either system (no D-test supported), the paper reports acquisition as a secondary section whose finding is that where the correction is placed matters; the further statement that which points are chosen does not is made only if the fit-to-fit CV measured in the pilot (4a) is below 10%, otherwise the result is reported as inconclusive at this fit budget; Koermer et al. (arXiv:2301.10228) is prior evidence for such a null. No further acquisition analysis is added after seeing results.

## 5. Alignment range: definition and secondary predictions (dev-informed)

**Definition** (code: `mismatch/alignment.py`). A dictionary of nine hypothesised missing terms h(phi, omega): omega, omega|omega|, omega^3, tanh(omega/0.1), tanh(omega/0.5), phi, phi^3, sin 2phi, phi omega. For each term, the linear response of the nominal trajectory (finite perturbation of size 1e-3). The alignment range of a signal is the minimum and maximum Spearman correlation between the signal and the relative response norm over the single terms and over 2,000 random signed mixtures of 2 to 3 terms (each term scaled to unit median response). It uses no true-system data.

**R1 (bracketing, full dictionary).** For nominal ensemble sigma and the nominal-ODE residual, at 16 x eps_ref and n_nom = 512, on each system, the Spearman correlation between the signal and the true discrepancy norm lies inside the alignment range computed with the full dictionary, in at least 9 of 10 seeds for each of the 4 (signal, system) combinations. Secondary, uncorrected, reported as a count with exact binomial intervals. It supports "a simulator-only signal cannot be certified without real data", not a prediction of the sign.

**R1s (strict, family-informed; replaces the earlier "true family removed" bracket).** At 4 x and 16 x eps_ref, n_nom = 512, the same correlation is within **0.20** of the mean predicted correlation of the true-family terms (S1: omega|omega| and omega^3; S2: tanh(omega/0.1) and tanh(omega/0.5)), where the predicted value uses only the simulator. Required in at least 9 of 10 seeds in each of the 8 (signal, system, level) combinations. The tolerance is dev-informed: on three development seeds the largest gap was 0.01 on S1 and 0.13 on S2, over three mismatch levels and two signals (36 combinations), so 0.20 leaves a margin of 0.07. Secondary, uncorrected.

**R1c (descriptive, true family removed).** Report in how many seeds the actual correlation lies inside the min-max range computed without the true family, and inside its 5th to 95th percentile band over mixtures. Expected to fail for ensemble sigma on S1 at 16 x: on the development seeds the 5-95% band contained the actual value in 0 of 3 seeds, and the min-max range in 1 of 3, because the remaining terms reach about 0.66 to 0.71 against an actual 0.65 to 0.72 to 0.75. This is why bracketing is not made stricter by narrowing the band.

**R2 (width, descriptive).** Report the width of each range. The development-seed widths are about 1.3-1.4 on a scale whose maximum is 2, which makes bracketing easy; R1 therefore supports the claim "a simulator-only signal cannot be certified without real data", not "the range predicts the sign". The paper will say so.

**R3 (hypothesis selection from real trajectories; two secondary tests per system).** At 4 x eps_ref, n_nom = 512, from the first n real trajectories of the calibration stream, compute the debiased discrepancy norm of each (v1.1 section 4) and its Spearman correlation with each dictionary term's response norm at the same settings; the term with the highest correlation is selected. The test statistic is the Spearman correlation between the selected term's response norm and the true discrepancy norm on the 500 test points.
- **R3a (family in the dictionary).** At n = 16, the statistic exceeds that of nominal ensemble sigma in at least 9 of 10 seeds and is at least 0.90 in at least 9 of 10 seeds. Development seeds (3): at n = 8 the selected term is already omega|omega| on S1 and tanh(omega/0.1) on S2 in 3 of 3 seeds, with statistics 1.00 and 0.99 against 0.65-0.72 and -0.09 to -0.14 for ensemble sigma; the selection did not change at n = 16 or 32.
- **R3b (true family removed from the dictionary).** At n = 16, the statistic exceeds that of ensemble sigma in at least 9 of 10 seeds. Development seeds: selected terms p^3 (S1) and sin 2phi (S2) with statistics +0.84 and +0.62 against +0.65 to +0.72 and -0.09 to -0.14, so the margins are 0.12 to 0.19 on S1 and about 0.7 on S2.
- Caveats stated now: the dictionary was written by the author knowing the two true families; omega, omega|omega|, omega^3 and phi^3 are nearly collinear on S1, so "selects the right family" is not tested, only the correlation; S2 truth uses tanh(omega/0.05) while the dictionary holds tanh(omega/0.1) and tanh(omega/0.5), so it is close to but not exactly in the dictionary; 1% observation noise; three development seeds only. Selection from n = 8 to 32 trajectories also feeds the paper's answer to "how many real experiments" (D3 of v1.5 remains as frozen).

## 6. Cut order and schedule

There is no fixed end date; the aim is to finish as early as the steps allow. **Cut now (decided and approved 2026-11-15, before any registered-seed result):** model B and the S2 direction prediction P-B (v1.1 section 3); synthetic-powered intervals and tests C2 and C3, with the Theorem 3.5 bounds (v1.1 section 5, v1.2 section 6), so Family C reduces to C1; the k-curve main run and the exact error decomposition (v1.1 section 7); arm 9 (v1.2 section 5); the semigroup signal E1 (v1.3); the extra n_nom acquisition cells; and the 5% noise analysis. These are reported as not run, with this reason. Beyond that, if compute or time falls short, analyses are removed in this order, each recorded in section 11 of `preregistration.md` before the affected results are looked at: arm 9; G3 (measured amplification); SPI and test C2; P-B (the S2 direction of H4); G2 beyond the pendulum; Family F arm F6. Not cut: Families A, B and C tests that remain computable, RQ1 with its seven signals, the alignment range.

**Compute note for Family F (estimate from dev timings).** One cell (one seed, one system) needs about 59 fits across the five arms at about 20 s each, so about 20 minutes, or about 6.6 CPU-hours for 10 seeds and 2 systems. If compute falls short, the term-disagreement arm, which needs 5 of every 6 fits, is cut first within Family F and tests F1-F2 are reported as not run.
