# Week 1 novelty check (2026-11-04)

Two alphaXiv searches (multi-fidelity active learning for neural surrogates; multi-fidelity under model discrepancy), then full-text reads of 9 papers. One item (Jaber et al. SSRN 7272715) could not be fetched.

**Gate verdict: passed, provisionally.** None of the 9 papers sweeps the strength of a missing simulator term and measures both (a) whether nominal-only uncertainty or residual signals keep ranking the true error and (b) whether they still guide which true-system experiments to run, against a space-filling design. The pieces exist separately, and two of them change the design (see "Design changes" below).

## Design changes from this check (add to the pre-registration)

1. **Wording of H\*.** Ensemble blindness to shared bias is textbook. Claim the measured dose-response in r, the matched transition points, and the effect on acquisition, not the blindness itself.
2. **Contrast against ε = 0.** Greedy residual and error acquisition can lose to random or Sobol with no mismatch (2610.27130). Primary RQ2 contrasts stay as slopes of the arm-vs-Sobol gap against log r, which removes the ε = 0 offset.
3. **Metrics.** RQ1 adds AUSE (sparsification) to AUROC and Spearman, and reports calibration separately from ranking.
4. **Smoothness proxy.** Report an H¹-seminorm ratio of the discrepancy alongside r, since S1 and S2 differ in smoothness at equal size.
5. **Terminology.** Use "structural simulator mismatch" or "missing term", never "model-form uncertainty".
6. **Citations to add:** 2602.01176 (as withdrawn), 2604.18045 and 2502.00550 (arm 5), 2606.23215 (arm 7), 2610.35560 (RQ1 framing), 2610.27130 (residual acquisition can hurt), 2502.05372 (BED under discrepancy), 2608.04708 (MF degrades with gap), 2608.03360 (terminology).
